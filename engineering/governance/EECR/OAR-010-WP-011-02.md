## Connector Architecture Compliance

WP-011-02 enforces the connector-as-translator invariant structurally:

- `SCADAEventTranslator` produces only `OperationalEvent` objects (OA-070 v1.0);
- `IngestionClient` submits them to the Phase 1 `OperationalEventProcessor`;
- `ConnectorPipeline` records outcomes only;
- no business logic, no control output, no write-back, no device command surfaces
  exist anywhere in the connector package.

The framework is read-only by construction, satisfying OA-072 and the
WP-011-01 security architecture specification.
