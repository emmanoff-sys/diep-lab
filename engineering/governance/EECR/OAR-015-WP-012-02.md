## WP-012-02 Architecture Compliance

WP-012-02 satisfies the PAO-030 engineering standards:

- `StateEstimationService` resides canonically in
  `services/adms_grid_analytics/state_estimation_service.py`. All estimation
  mathematics delegate to `state_estimation.estimate()` — no algorithm is
  implemented or duplicated in the service module.

- Measurement processing (`process_measurements`) operates at the service
  boundary only: validates node existence, coerces numeric types, rejects
  unknown nodes and non-numeric values, and reports coverage statistics.

- Topology validation (`validate_topology`) raises `ValueError` for hard
  errors (no substation, dangling edge references) and records warnings for
  soft issues (all switches open). No estimation proceeds on invalid topology.

- The WP-007 adapter (`_nodes_edges_from_snapshot`) converts `TopologySnapshot`
  typed objects to engine-compatible plain dicts. The WP-008 adapter
  (`_measurements_from_op_state`) extracts `{p_kw, q_kvar, voltage_pu}` from
  `OperationalAssetState.attributes`. Neither adapter requires live platform
  services (test-friendly constructor injection).

- `GridAnalyticsService.estimate_state()` constructs `StateEstimationService`
  internally and delegates the call, preserving the existing caller-facing
  signature (nodes, edges, measurements, snapshot, op_state, options).

- `contracts.py` is extended with `StateEstimationConfig`, `MeasurementSummary`,
  `TopologyValidation` TypedDicts (under `TYPE_CHECKING`), and `EstimationResult`
  is extended with WP-012-02 enrichment fields.

- PAO-030 OUT OF SCOPE constraints satisfied: no new estimation algorithm,
  no power flow changes, no contingency analysis, no Volt/VAR, no forecasting,
  no ML, no Digital Twin, no operator workflow changes, no external connector
  changes, no runtime redesign.
