## Validation Requirements

WP-009 validation covers:

- outage detection over live operational state (dark components, source loss,
  feeder attribution, candidate grouping);
- isolation boundary discovery, operability filtering, and simulated
  isolation verification with leak evidence;
- switching plan generation with safety rules SR-001..SR-005, precondition
  validation against live state, and rollback sequences;
- restoration candidate analysis with capacity-aware deterministic ranking;
- operator decision support with advisories and plain-language explanations;
- append-only operational audit trail with transitive traceability and
  operator acknowledgement;
- end-to-end integration from WP-008 operational events to traceable
  recommendations, including determinism;
- WP-006/WP-007/WP-008 regression;
- existing CIM/topology regression;
- static analysis and formatting gates.
