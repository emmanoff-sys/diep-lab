## WP-012-03 Architecture Compliance

- `PowerFlowService` resides canonically in `services/adms_grid_analytics/power_flow_service.py`. All power flow mathematics delegate to `powerflow.solve()` — confirmed by source scan (no `SBASE_1PH_KW`, no `i_load`, no `SLACK`, no `converged` flag in service module).
- SE result drives load profile (OA-114): `loads_from_se_result()` converts per-node balanced `estimated_p_kw/q_kvar` to per-phase `complex(P, Q)` divided equally across active phases. Explicit `loads` argument takes precedence.
- `validate_se_consistency()` checks node-set completeness and topology validity before any power flow executes; inconsistency raises `ValueError`.
- `GridAnalyticsService.solve_power_flow()` now accepts `se_result` and delegates to `PowerFlowService`. Caller-facing signature is backward-compatible (`loads` still accepted).
- PAO-031 OUT OF SCOPE constraints satisfied: no contingency analysis, no Volt/VAR, no optimal power flow, no reconfiguration, no ML, no forecasting.
