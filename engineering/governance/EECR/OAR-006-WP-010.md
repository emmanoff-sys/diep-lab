governance-readiness state, and Programme Board readiness for the analytical
decision services foundation work package.

## Governance Rules

The Objective Acceptance Register is an engineering governance artefact.

It shall:

- record implementation and validation progress only;
- preserve objective-level traceability;
- support GOV-002 review;
- support Programme Board acceptance;
- avoid implementation detail beyond summary-level evidence.

Historical entries shall remain immutable after acceptance unless superseded by
a formally authorised correction.
