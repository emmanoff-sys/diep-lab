## GIS Adapter Architecture Compliance

WP-011-03 enforces the connector-as-translator invariant structurally:

- `GISTopologyTranslator` produces only canonical `MappedTopology` objects
  (OA-070 v1.0); `validate_mapped_topology()` is the acceptance gate;
- `TopologyReconciler` is advisory-only; it produces diff reports but makes
  no correction to the imported topology or the existing model;
- `GISAssetIdentityMap` validates GIS-to-canonical identity mappings at
  construction time (fail-fast per OA-069 §8);
- `GISConnectorSession` extends `AbstractConnectorSession` without reimplementing
  any framework primitive from WP-011-02;
- no business logic, no write-back, no GIS modification, no control output, and
  no command surface exist anywhere in the connector package.

The adapter is read-only by construction, consistent with the WP-011-01 security
architecture specification (OA-072) and the connector-as-translator pattern (OA-069).
