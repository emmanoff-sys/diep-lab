## WP-012-04 Architecture Compliance

- `ContingencyAnalysisService` resides canonically in `services/adms_grid_analytics/contingency_analysis_service.py`. All N-1 analysis mathematics delegate to `contingency.analyze()` — confirmed by source scan (no `_energized`, `_restore`, `_is_radial`, `copy.deepcopy` in service module).
- SE→CA load derivation (OA-120): `_loads_from_se_result()` delegates to injected `PowerFlowService.loads_from_se_result()` when available; otherwise uses an inline fallback implementing the identical algorithm (converts per-node balanced `estimated_p_kw/q_kvar` to per-phase `complex(P/n_ph, Q/n_ph)` across active phases). Explicit `loads` argument takes precedence over SE-derived loads.
- Impact summary (OA-121/122): `_impact_summary()` is a pure aggregation over the engine result — no solver logic.
- `GridAnalyticsService.analyze_contingency()` now accepts `se_result` and `load_floor` and delegates to `ContingencyAnalysisService`. Caller-facing signature is backward-compatible (`loads` still accepted).
- PAO-032 OUT OF SCOPE constraints satisfied: no automatic switching, no FLISR, no operator execution, no protection coordination, no Volt/VAR, no optimal power flow, no ML, no forecasting.
