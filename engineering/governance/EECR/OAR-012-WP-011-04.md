## AMI Connector Architecture Compliance

WP-011-04 enforces the connector-as-translator invariant structurally:

- `AMIEventTranslator` produces only canonical `OperationalEvent` objects
  (OA-070 v1.0); `validate_operational_event()` is the acceptance gate;
- `AMIMeterIdentityMap` validates AMI-to-canonical identity mappings at
  construction time (fail-fast per OA-069 §8);
- `AMIConnectorSession` extends `AbstractConnectorSession` from WP-011-02
  without reimplementing any framework primitive;
- `AMIIngestionAdapter` wraps WP-011-02 `IngestionClient` and adds meter-ID
  correlation to each submission record without reimplementing trust-boundary
  enforcement;
- no business logic, no write-back, no meter control, no command surface, and
  no AMI modification capability exist anywhere in the connector package.

The connector is read-only by construction, consistent with the WP-011-01
security architecture specification (OA-072) and the connector-as-translator
pattern (OA-069).
