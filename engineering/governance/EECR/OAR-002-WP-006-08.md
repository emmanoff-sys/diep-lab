## Objective Completion Criteria

Each objective may only transition to Accepted when all of the following are
complete:

- implementation remains limited to the authorised objective;
- no unrelated refactoring or unauthorised functionality is introduced;
- required local validation is complete;
- engineering review has accepted the evidence;
- GOV-002 review has accepted the evidence;
- Programme Board has accepted the objective or explicitly recorded conditions.

## Validation Requirements

Each objective shall complete, where applicable:

- compile validation;
- unit tests;
- integration tests;
- topology regression;
- CIM regression;
- Ruff;
- Black;
- isort;
- Bandit;
- `git diff --check`.

Warnings, unavailable local tools, and Release Engineering follow-up actions
shall be documented separately.
